Goals & limits

Shown on the Overview and Risk pages. When today's losses reach the limit, a red banner appears on every page.

Trading fees

Your exchange's fee rates, as a % of the trade's value. They fill in automatically when you log or close a trade by hand — a Long buys on entry and sells on exit, a Short sells on entry and buys back on exit. You can still edit the fee on any trade. Changing these affects trades you log from now on; earlier trades keep the fees they were saved with. Bybit-synced trades always use Bybit's actual fees.

Bybit sync

Trades sync from Bybit when you press Sync, and — with auto-sync on — whenever the app is open (every 3 minutes, and when you come back to the tab).
